Bio

I am a Senior Member of Technical Staff at Holiday Robotics. I study how agents reason, choose actions, and improve from feedback, drawing on my research in efficient LLM inference, adaptive reasoning, and reinforcement learning.

At Holiday Robotics, I develop agent harnesses with planning, spatial perception, and act–verify loops, alongside harness learning with dense feedback. I also work on text-and-motion-conditioned RL policies, robot data flywheels, and embodied-agent benchmarks. Previously, I led agentic search and LLM alignment at LG AI Research, combining planning, tool use, retrieval, and post-training in a system for expert-domain tasks.

My published work spans adaptive thinking, instruction-guided decoding, speculative and parallel decoding, and knowledge distillation. I received my Ph.D. in AI from KAIST, advised by Prof. Se-Young Yun, and interned at Google Research, Dynamo AI, and Qualcomm AI.

Research Focus

My research connects efficient reasoning, agent execution, and learning from feedback, with a current focus on embodied agents. 효율적 추론과 에이전트의 행동, 피드백을 통한 학습을 연결하며, 현재는 이를 실제 환경에서 작업을 수행하는 embodied agent로 확장하고 있습니다.

  • Harness development and learning

    I am developing agent execution harnesses with planning, perception for spatial intelligence, and act–verify loops. Alongside this, I work on meta-harness-style training with dense feedback to guide harness learning.

  • From text and motion to RL action policies

    I developed an algorithm that automates manual engineering in constructing RL action policies from text and motion. Behavior-cloning regularization helps the policies follow intended behaviors.

  • Data flywheel

    I work on a robot data flywheel, including grasping and video-based progress labeling across real-world and simulated robot data.

  • Embodied-agent evaluation

    I am building benchmarks for embodied-agent behavior and task execution. This evaluation work is in progress.

  • Efficient reasoning and post-training

    My published work studies when models should reason, how to guide answer generation, and how speculative and parallel decoding can reduce inference cost. My training experience includes SFT, DPO, and GRPO-based methods.
