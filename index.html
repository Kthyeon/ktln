Bio

I am a Senior Member of Technical Staff at Holiday Robotics. My research focuses on efficient LLM inference and adaptive reasoning: how models allocate computation, generate answers, and learn when to reason.

My work spans adaptive thinking, instruction-guided decoding, and parallel and speculative decoding. At Holiday Robotics, I am extending this research to embodied agents that use robot skills and improve through execution feedback.

I received my Ph.D. in AI from KAIST (카이스트), advised by Prof. Se-Young Yun in the OSI Lab. Previously, I worked at LG AI Research and interned at Google Research, Dynamo AI, and Qualcomm AI.

I love coffee, running, and long walks at Seoul Forest, and I enjoy the Seongsu neighborhood culture. 커피를 좋아하고, 러닝과 서울숲 산책을 즐기며 성수동 문화를 좋아합니다.

Research Focus

I study how models reason effectively with less computation, and am extending this work to agents that act in the physical world. 더 적은 연산으로 효과적으로 추론하는 방법을 연구하며, 이를 실제 환경에서 행동하는 에이전트로 확장하고 있습니다.

  • Efficient inference and adaptive reasoning

    Learning when to reason and accelerating answer generation through adaptive thinking, parallel decoding, and speculative decoding.

  • Learning from feedback

    Using reinforcement learning, evaluation, and adaptive training data to improve reasoning quality and computational efficiency.

  • Current direction: embodied agents

    At Holiday Robotics, I work on agents that select and compose robot skills. My focus includes evaluation, synthetic robot trajectories, and automatic labeling of real-world data to support policy improvement.
